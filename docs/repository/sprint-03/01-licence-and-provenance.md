The revival has an open-source direction but no settled project licence or complete provenance decision for the material to be distributed. A versioned package must not turn that intention into an unsupported licence claim.

## Scope

- Identify the new package's exact source, dependency, font, logo and other asset inventory, keeping historical material outside the package separate.
- Record existing notices and unresolved rights questions without removing third-party attribution or inferring permission from public GitHub visibility.
- Prepare a concise licence/contribution-terms proposal and the decisions requiring the product owner's confirmation.
- After an authorized decision, add the applicable licence text, notices and contributor guidance consistently with that decision.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The shipped-material inventory names versions, provenance and relevant notices; unresolved items have an explicit inclusion/exclusion decision.
- [ ] The product owner's licence decision and its scope are recorded. No licence is selected automatically to close this issue.
- [ ] Release files and public wording match the decision, including attribution obligations and any remaining exclusions.
- [ ] Contribution guidance explains the applicable terms without inventing a grant for historical code.
- [ ] Package publication is blocked while a required rights/licence decision remains unresolved; engineering preparation may continue independently.

## Boundary

This issue produces a reviewed decision and release materials, not a legal certification. It does not authorize altering third-party licences or accepting terms on another person's behalf.
