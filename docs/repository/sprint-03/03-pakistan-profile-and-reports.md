Current income/expense and position summaries do not provide a reviewed accounting framework, complete classifications or reproducible professional statements. Build a narrowly scoped first Pakistan profile and report engine driven by accounting requirements, not merely by a new visual format.

## Scope

- Agree one entity/profile with a qualified accounting reviewer, recording legal form, applicable framework/edition, effective period, policy choices and exclusions. Do not infer the profile from currency or IP location.
- Maintain a source-to-rule register covering recognition, measurement, classifications, posting/adjustment/closing behavior and the statement/disclosure requirements included in the candidate.
- Add reviewed, versioned account-to-report mappings with explicit current/non-current treatment where relevant, genuine comparison periods and visible completeness states.
- Use the original two-year retail fixture as a reproducible acceptance case, translating its supplied opening/adjustment outcomes through legitimate accounting services. Preserve its distinction between fixture inputs and implemented operational modules or tax calculations.
- Present statements with clear dates, scale/currency, subtotals, source drilldowns and the profile/version used.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The profile and included rules have recorded accounting review; unresolved decisions remain visible before affected behavior is enabled.
- [ ] Unmapped accounts, unavailable supporting data and incomplete openings cannot silently become zero or a completed statement. Nonnumeric section headings remain distinct from missing numeric values.
- [ ] The original fixture reconciles through actual services, including two-period profit, position and equity; include cash flow/notes only with the selected profile's required data and reviewed policy.
- [ ] The fixture retains exact values in PKR thousands: profit 504/392, assets 1,940/1,550, retained earnings 704/300 and closing cash 650/500. Display scaling does not change stored amounts. Its supplied tax amounts are not a tax engine.
- [ ] Supplied inventory/COGS fixture outcomes are not represented as a working inventory-costing module; reports requiring absent subledger data are marked unavailable or preliminary.
- [ ] Closing/profit presentation avoids double counting, and corrections preserve posted history. Report totals reconcile to source journals within company/book scope.
- [ ] Mapping/profile changes cannot silently rewrite an issued result; its sources, period and version can be reproduced.
- [ ] Representative owner/accountant review covers readability, drilldown and missing-data explanations. Technical tests alone do not establish compliance.

## Boundary

Pakistan is first; UK/UAE and broader entity profiles remain later work. This issue does not promise complete regulatory coverage or install AR/AP, inventory, payroll or tax modules merely to populate a template. Narrow the candidate's supported statements if their dependencies are incomplete.
