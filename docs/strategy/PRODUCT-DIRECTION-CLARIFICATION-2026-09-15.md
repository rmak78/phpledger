# Product direction clarification — 15 September 2026

The owner's direct clarification after the evening strategy update supersedes the earlier interpretation that Pakistan was the primary audience or ultimate direction. This is the controlling product statement for subsequent implementation and publication.

- PHP Ledger is a country-neutral, self-hosted accounting core for small businesses, owners, bookkeepers, accountants and multi-company operators across countries.
- Pakistan is one intended regional direction. Its researched SME/legal-entity examples do not define the entire audience or impose a Pakistan-first launch on other countries.
- Pakistan tax/FBR digital invoicing is one planned connector. Regional accounting profiles, tax/e-invoicing connectors and their credentials/rules stay outside the generic posting core and activate only for the supported entity, period and jurisdiction.
- FBR, ZATCA, UAE Peppol PINT and Oman are distinct adapter targets. Other regional research remains useful; readiness and validated demand determine connector delivery rather than a fixed global country queue.
- Owner-equity reporting, exact accounting, phone-friendly entry, traceable sources, scoped access, open-source licensing and optional business modules serve the broader product.
- The existing Urdu-then-Arabic/RTL language plan and server-only posting rule remain; a translation or currency option does not establish regional accounting/tax compliance.
- The owner authorized resolving the PHP compatibility issue and publishing the validated corrections. PHP 8.2 remains the minimum target, with PHP 8.3 the deployment default; no requirement above PHP 8.3 is imposed. Runtime, package and live evidence are recorded separately.

The supplied earlier strategy prompt/register and published release receipts retain their history. Current README, product brief, architecture, module roadmap and release copy must follow this clarification. No connector or new financial feature is implemented merely by this document.
