The proposed type rules

Manrope at 600–700 for headings. Poppins at 400 for regular text, starting at 16px with a comfortable line height. Inter for amounts, prices, totals, percentages and numeric table columns, with tabular numerals and aligned decimals.

Use this spacious rhythm for onboarding and owner screens. Reports and POS need tighter, task-specific layouts. Urdu and Arabic will require a companion font with the appropriate glyphs and shaping support.
