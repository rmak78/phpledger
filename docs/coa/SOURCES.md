## Important interpretation limits

- The IFRS for SMEs overview says the 2025 edition is effective for periods starting 1 January 2027 with early adoption described. Local adoption and the company's reporting eligibility still require review; the wizard must not choose the edition from today's date alone. (SRC-IFRS-SME)
- The inspected Odoo PK and IN charts contain rows/descriptions that merit review before any reuse. Upstream account classifications are not automatically the intended PHP Ledger classification. See [discovery gaps](DISCOVERY_GAPS.md).
- A generic FBR overview found during discovery included historical rate/threshold language and was excluded as a current rules source. A historical ICAP handbook result was also excluded from current applicability claims.
- No search-result mirror, forum post, marketing article, or third-party accounting blog is used as authority for country requirements in this package.
- Country regulations, legal-form reporting, industry operations, and actual migration sample files require more work. This is a documented research base for the installer, not completed regulatory localization.
