# Sample-data inspiration sources

Research date: **2026-09-14**. The following are public vendor-authored marketplace descriptions or vendor documentation. They were used to identify useful business situations, not to verify product quality, security, accounting correctness or PHP Ledger compatibility. Public descriptions can change and may overstate capabilities. No product was purchased, installed or logged into; no live demo transaction was submitted.

| Source ID | Public reference | Observed pattern and application to our original pack |
|---|---|---|
| `cc-tabletrack` | [TableTrack on CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/item/tabletrack-the-complete-saas-restaurant-management-solution/55116396) | Listing identifies table, menu, order, KOT and payment screens. Inspired a linked table order/kitchen ticket/payment scenario |
| `vendor-tabletrack` | [TableTrack public demo landing](https://tabletrack.froid.works/?show=demo_landing) | Vendor landing describes POS/KOT, QR ordering, reservations and reports. Read-only landing access; authenticated operations were not inspected |
| `cc-tribu` | [Tribu club-management listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/tribu-club-management-android-and-ios-app/62967899) | Describes membership approval, renewal payments, expiry and attendance. Inspired dues/renewal fixtures; it does not establish the revenue-recognition policy used in our example |
| `cc-medicine-care` | [Medicine Care pharmacy listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/pharmacy-management-software-laravel/36422063) | Describes batch/expiry inventory, partial payments, returns and cash accounts. Inspired traceable lots and unpaid supplier balances using non-medicinal dummy items |
| `cc-pharmacy-pims` | [Pharmacy Management System listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/pharmacy-management-system/33700134) | Describes low-stock and date-range expiry views. Inspired a fixed-date near-expiry task; no clinical features or medicine records were copied |
| `cc-ultimate-pos` | [Ultimate POS listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/ultimate-pos-stock-management-point-of-sale-application/21216332) | Public release notes describe price groups, customer/supplier opening balances, stock transfers, returns and credit limits. Inspired trader price tiers and opening-detail reconciliation |
| `cc-stocky` | [Stocky POS listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/stockyultimate-inventory-management-system-with-pos/31445124) | Describes retail/wholesale, warehouses, repairs and quantity pricing. Used to select varied sample-business situations; no architecture or vendor feature list was adopted wholesale |
| `vendor-stocky` | [Stocky vendor documentation](https://demo.getstocky.com/documentation/index.html) | Documents product units, warehouse/van locations, parts on service jobs, subscriptions, and isolated demo catalogs. Inspired compact onboarding examples, a customer repair asset and separate sample/real-company paths. Vendor implementation instructions are not PHP Ledger instructions |
| `odoo-marketplace-distribution` | [Third-party WT Sale Distributor listing on Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/wt_sale_distributor) | Search-indexed vendor text describes routes/stops, deliveries and collections. Direct page retrieval failed. This is a third-party listing, not Odoo core documentation or a tested product. Our van settlement is an original proposal |

The [Repairer CodeCanyon listing](https://codecanyon.net/item/repairer-repairworkshop-management-system/18913244) was also found, but direct retrieval timed out and search evidence was too limited for detailed workflow claims. The workshop pack relies on the accessible Stocky vendor documentation plus original scenario design. No missing evidence was replaced with a claim that its demo was tested.

## Licensing and reuse boundary

The [CodeCanyon license overview](https://codecanyon.net/licenses/standard) describes Regular and Extended commercial end-product licenses. Marketplace availability is not permission to redistribute a product's code, screenshots, sample database, logo or paid assets inside an open-source repository. No license was acquired and no proprietary source, downloaded package, demo customer record, screenshot or vendor branding was copied into these packs.

All fictional company names, contacts, IDs, catalog descriptions, quantities, documents and financial examples were created for PHP Ledger. The project-wide open-source license/provenance decision remains in [LICENSE_REVIEW.md](../LICENSE_REVIEW.md); this research does not choose that license or incorporate marketplace license terms into the project.

References supply business-workflow inspiration only. Expected journals are our explicit proposed accounting model, awaiting independent accountant approval. Public product descriptions do not validate opening-balance methods, tax status, inventory costing, pharmacy regulations or club revenue recognition. No Google Drive documents, real customer databases, payment providers or private account surfaces were used.
