# Licensing and contributions

Project-owned core code and current modules in 1.0.0 use **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. The accounting core remains open source. The pre-adoption 0.1.0 through 0.1.5 previews retain their original MIT grants; third-party dependencies, assets and marks keep their own terms.

A separate commercial licence is available on request for uses outside AGPL obligations. Future commercial modules will be named before release; none is declared by this release. Desktop/Android clients, a consultant edition, an unposted khata add-on and a hosted FBR relay remain candidates.

Self-hosting for your own business is free, without licence keys or licensing-server calls. Hosting, setup, training and support are optional services.

New contributions require the [individual/entity CLA](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/blob/master/CLA.md). Contributors retain copyright and grant the permissions needed for AGPL distribution and commercial sublicensing. The pinned CLA Assistant workflow stores signatures on a separate branch; a successful hosted signature/recheck must be observed before claiming that flow verified. No signature is assumed or backdated.

Read [LICENSE](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/blob/master/LICENSE), [licence scope](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/blob/master/LICENSE-SCOPE.md), [licensing policy](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/blob/master/docs/LICENSING-POLICY.md) and [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/phpledger/phpledger/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
