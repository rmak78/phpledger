# PHP Ledger 0.5.0 vertical account research

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** local research-backed sample seed catalogue  
**Scope:** the eleven selected sample companies, using the local SMB vertical matrix and additional chart/workflow references.

## Finding

The matrix is broad but the previous samples were too shallow at the chart
level. They mostly exposed one generic sales account, one inventory account,
and one cost account. That loses the distinctions an owner or bookkeeper needs
to explain gross margin, cash timing, stock controls, and the boundary between
owned goods and customer property.

The new `resources/coa/industry-profiles-0.5.0.json` catalogue adds a small,
seedable account vocabulary for each selected sample. It records stable
semantic keys, illustrative default codes, fixture roles, normal balance,
intended posting purpose, future operational controls, unsupported operations,
and source URLs with the concept each source supports.

The numeric codes are presentation defaults only. They are not national or
statutory account numbers. Country tax packages remain separate.

## Research basis

The local matrix remains the breadth and Pakistan-market anchor. The added
references deepen the account and workflow concepts without importing another
product's chart or code into PHP Ledger:

- The supplied [IFRS/US GAAP standardized chart](https://www.ifrs-gaap.com/standardized-chart-accounts)
  supplies a useful general vocabulary for assets, liabilities, equity,
  income, expenses, contra accounts, and inventory-related headings.
- [ERPNext accounting](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-introduction),
  [accounting entries](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-entries),
  [purchase-cycle ledger impact](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/purchase-cycle-ledger-impact),
  and [inventory accounting](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-of-inventory-stock)
  establish the separation between commercial documents, stock movements,
  source documents, and generated GL entries.
- [ERPNext POS profiles](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/pos-profile) support
  the retail distinction between cash/POS controls, warehouses, payment
  methods, and write-offs.
- [ERPNext BOMs](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/bill-of-materials) and
  [work orders](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/work-order) support the future
  manufacturing distinctions between raw material, WIP, operations, scrap,
  and finished goods.
- [Odoo expiration dates](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/product_management/product_tracking/expiration_dates.html)
  and [ERPNext batch inventory](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/managing-batch-wise-inventory)
  support the pharmacy-style lot/expiry vocabulary. They do not make the
  PHP Ledger sample a pharmacy system.
- The [ACNC National Standard Chart of Accounts](https://www.acnc.gov.au/for-charities/manage-your-charity/national-standard-chart-accounts)
  supplies the membership-club distinctions around dues, events, restricted
  and unrestricted funds, and functional reporting.
- The practitioner examples for [agencies](https://cocountant.com/blog/bookkeeping/what-should-my-agency-chart-of-accounts-look-like/),
  [landscaping](https://www.youraspire.com/templates/landscaping/chart-of-accounts),
  [restaurants](https://knowledge-base.mesa.cpa/chart-of-accounts-restaurant-cafe),
  [jewelry](https://accountingforjewelers.com/track-and-price-jewelry-inventory/),
  and [repair shops](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/08/21/auto-repair-shop-bookkeeping-parts-inventory-labor-rate-comeback-warranty-guide)
  provide practical revenue/cost splits and operational caveats.
- HysabOne's Pakistan vertical pages for [FMCG](https://hysabone.com/industries/fmcg/),
  [pharma distribution](https://hysabone.com/industries/pharma-distribution/),
  [media](https://hysabone.com/industries/media/), and [automobile](https://hysabone.com/industries/automobile/)
  are treated as vendor workflow claims and market signals, not independent
  compliance proof.

## Runtime boundary

The builder embeds the selected profile into each successor pack's pinned
research evidence. Isolated sample provisioning reads the profile's
`seed_role_labels` when creating company-owned operational accounts, so a
restaurant does not display the same generic account labels as a workshop.
The event stream, central posting services, duplicate keys, opening-detail
handling, and company isolation remain the source of financial behavior.

Profiles do not add a second database, posting path, or automatic tax setup.
Their accounts are not automatically installed into real companies, and
unsupported operations remain staged in the evidence contract.
