- **Module and API/MCP foundation:** the bundled core/POS lifecycle is implemented locally with versioned contracts, company capability gates, compatibility checks and retained history. Shared contacts/catalog and tax are reserved contracts for their consuming modules. Next add versioned business API and MCP reads, then scoped, revocable, audited commands through the same accounting services. No public business API or MCP server is implemented yet.
- **Optional AR and AP:** invoices/bills, receipts/payment recording, allocation, credit notes, customer/vendor statements and aging, each reconciling its open items to core control accounts. Customer/vendor identities are roles on shared contacts. These follow core and extension/access foundations; they do not define the minimum core installation.
- **Multi-book and localization:** first define separate companies versus alternative reporting bases and traceable reconciliation rules. Country accounting/reporting now follows the approved Pakistan → UK → UAE priority above, with versioned entity/period profiles and separately reviewed country/industry templates and tax adapters. India remains a later candidate from the seed brief. No region has current regulatory coverage; review each jurisdiction's recognition, measurement, presentation and tax obligations independently and keep its policy out of the generic posting core.
- **Multilingual, time, and formatting:** English remains the primary/fallback catalog. Externalize interface text, preserve UTF-8, and review additional translations, script fonts, and future RTL screens before declaring language support. Store event/audit instants in UTC and format them using each terminal's IANA timezone; posting/fiscal/cutover DATE values remain unchanged. Add flexible date/currency presentation, symbol/code placement, Western (`123,456,789`) and South Asian (`12,34,56,789`) grouping, separators, and precision within accounting constraints. Use locale defaults with user/terminal overrides, and later company defaults for official exported documents. Validate explicit localized input without guessing ambiguous dates/numbers; keep exact decimal accounting. Test different terminal zones and daylight-saving transitions without shifting accounting dates or stored amounts.
- **Multicurrency:** introduce transaction currency alongside company/base-book currency, supporting fixed, periodically fetched, and manually overridden rates. Snapshot each posting's rate, source, and effective time and preserve posted history. Backend refresh must expose last-known-good/stale/failure states; overrides need permission and audit evidence. Decide provider, refresh cadence, cross-rate rules, conversion precision/rounding, and gains/revaluation behavior with accounting reviewers before implementation. Reconcile transaction and base amounts without using browser page loads for provider calls.
- **Inventory and purchasing:** products, warehouses, purchasing, stock movements, and accounting reconciliation. Select FIFO or weighted-average costing with accounting reviewers and pilot evidence before implementing valuation; do not silently mix methods. Tie stock valuation and cost of goods sold to journal sources.
- **Tax and production shop POS:** declare tax contracts before dependent document schemas; deliver the first reviewed Pakistan adapter before affected production transactions. Build beyond the cash-sale showcase with shared checkout and a shop interface. Non-stock/service checkout need not require inventory; stocked sales require quantity/costing, credit requires AR, and applicable tax cannot silently default to zero. Scope returns, tender reconciliation, hardware and end-of-day behavior with pilot users.
- **Restaurant POS:** add restaurant orders/tables, modifiers and kitchen workflows over the same checkout/catalog/accounting services. Bill splitting/merging, voids and optional recipes/ingredient costing have explicit contracts and separate user/accounting gates. A restaurant layout is not a complete restaurant module.
- **Van distribution:** mobile-friendly routes, van warehouses, stock handoffs, delivery/orders, on-site collections, and evening settlement. Stock and collected money must reconcile to warehouse and accounting records.
- **Specialist industries:** scope jewelry weight/stone/labor pricing and optional metal-price integration; pharmacy batch/expiry/prescription workflows; workshop jobs/parts/labor and membership dues as separate validated modules. Provider integrations and region-specific obligations require their own review and authorization.
- **Industry demonstration path:** maintain original, coherent sample companies for restaurants, membership clubs, pharmacies, traders, distributors, retail shops, and service workshops. Use them for setup previews, guided first tasks, tests, and public demonstrations once isolation exists. Club dues/renewals and workshop job cards/parts/labor are future module scenarios. Never mix a sample pack into a real company's books or claim that a scenario file implements its operational module. CodeCanyon product descriptions and public demos may inform workflow research; authored datasets and application code stay original.
- **Later delivery modes:** assess durable asynchronous operational events, offline synchronization, and Windows/mobile/Linux native wrappers only when measured operational needs justify them. Retain one authoritative accounting interface, duplicate protection, visible failed delivery, and reconciliation across modes.

These safeguards support the next phase; they are not sustained-load, independent security or regional compliance evidence. The [architecture's operational gates](ARCHITECTURE.md#capacity-security-and-operational-release-gates) separate existing controls from the following required work:

| Gate | Work and acceptance evidence |
|---|---|
| Concurrent checkout and reporting | Agree realistic terminals, history size and latency/error targets; run mixed checkout/report load and a sustained soak in same-book and separate-business cases. Record lock waits, retries, latency, resource use and reconciled totals. Publish no capacity claim before this evidence. |
| Measured report growth | Profile existing queries/indexes first. Introduce background reports or rebuildable summaries only where measured need justifies them, preserving source reconciliation, scope, dates and visible job status. |
| Independent security and privacy | Review the deployed application and hosting against a recorded OWASP ASVS scope; assess authentication/access, dependencies, backups and relevant data flows/retention. Passing current automated checks does not close this gate. |

| Offline POS, if demand is validated | Design durable local operation IDs and pending/acknowledged/rejected states; server deduplication and linked receipts; stock/price/permission/period conflicts; lost acknowledgements, restart, storage loss and reconciliation.
