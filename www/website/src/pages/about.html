Accounting you can inspect and host yourself

PHP Ledger is an open-source accounting project for small businesses, maintained by Rana Mansoor Akbar Khan with support from the BixiSoft community.

A useful accounting core comes first

The current {{site.release.version}} package records receipts, expenses and general journals through one double-entry posting service. Reports connect figures to their account movements and source records. Optional business modules build on that core.

The application uses PHP 8.2+, MySQL 8.4 and MeekroDB in BixiSoft's modular PHP structure. The project-owned core and current modules use AGPL-3.0-or-later; a commercial licence is available. Published 0.1.0 through 0.1.5 previews retain their MIT grants. Dependencies, fonts, photographs and historical source keep their own terms.

Country-neutral accounting, regional connectors

PHP Ledger is for small businesses, owners, bookkeepers and accountants across countries. Pakistan is one intended regional direction, not the main or ultimate product scope. Pakistan FBR is one planned connector alongside other tax and e-invoicing integrations. Each connector will have its own supported jurisdiction, entity and review requirements.

Free self-hosting and clear terms

The accounting core remains open source. Self-hosting for your own business is free, with no licence keys or licensing-server calls. A separate commercial licence is available on request. Future commercial modules will be named before release; native desktop and Android clients remain planned paid add-ons.

Built in the open

Contributors help improve accessibility, accounting checks, installation guidance and everyday workflows. Browse the source repository, Wiki and roadmap to see implemented work and remaining gates.

What preview means

Use synthetic records while evaluating the software. Qualified accounting review, jurisdiction-specific reporting, independent security review and observed business usability remain open gates. A balanced report alone does not establish compliance.
