The path from a working preview to supported books

The published package has a bounded accounting and cash-sale journey. Each next step needs reconciled examples, access checks and review before it becomes a supported capability.

In short

  • PHP Ledger {{site.release.version}} is an evaluation release with cash accounting workflows, reports and scoped read connections.
  • Party, currency, open-item and correction foundations are implemented; invoice and bill workflows are the next product milestone.
  • Inventory, statutory tax adapters, reviewed statement packages and supported business use each need separate delivery and acceptance evidence.

This roadmap describes development scope, not a delivery-date or regulatory-compliance promise.

Frequently asked questions

When will a stable release be available?

There is no committed stable-release date. A supported release needs reconciled accounting examples, qualified accounting review, observed usability, installation and recovery checks, and clear support scope.

Does choosing a country or currency activate local accounting rules?

No. Each book uses one base currency. Country and currency choices do not activate tax calculations, statutory reporting, foreign-currency posting or additional accounting books. The core is country-neutral. Pakistan FBR is one planned connector; each regional accounting or tax integration needs separate research, implementation and review.

How can I help shape the next release?

Share a synthetic workflow example, installation report or reproducible issue through the project repository. Accountants, business owners and contributors can also use the Support page to prepare an email about review or pilot participation.

Which parts are ready to evaluate?

Use the release boundary to choose a test task; a schema foundation does not mean the operator workflow is available.

How will the next milestone be accepted?

Examples must reconcile document, open-item and general-ledger balances, including partial payments and corrections.

  1. Make parties and contacts usable through permissioned forms.
  2. Build invoice and bill drafts through the existing posting service.
  3. Add allocations, settlement history and ageing from authoritative open items.
  4. Rehearse opening conversion, reversals, closed periods and concurrent retries.
  5. Obtain accounting and operator review before claiming supported business use.
