Double-entry accounting that runs on your own PHP and MySQL hosting

PHP Ledger connects invoices, bills, payments and optional stock workflows to balanced journals, then shows the profit and loss, balance sheet and cash position behind them. A cash sale at the counter becomes a receipt and a balanced journal

A cash sale creates a printable receipt and a journal through the central posting service.

What happens when a sale is posted?

Choose from the catalog, take the cash and print the receipt. The balanced entry records: 5.75 to cash, 5.75 to sales. Stock movement, tax and card payments are not included.

Compare the basket total with cash received and change before posting. In the synthetic screen, 10.00 received against a 5.75 sale leaves 4.25 change. This cash counter is useful for evaluating the posting path; it does not provide a tax invoice or a customer credit account.

Each expense keeps its payee, date, category and journal together

A draft stays outside the posted books until you review and post it.

Posting creates one balanced entry: 125.00 to general expenses, 125.00 from cash and bank. The source remains connected to its journal.

Why does a correction create a reversal?

A linked reversing entry preserves the original posted history and explains the change.

The correction foundation keeps the same document identity across reversal and reposting. Review the reason, reversal date and period controls instead of treating a posted amount as an editable cell. The correction lesson explains the accounting distinction.

At the end of the day the owner sees 875.00 in the bank and knows why

The reports read the same posted entries and connect their totals to the journal and source record.

How does the sample reach 875.00?

With no opening balance or other activity, 1,000.00 of cash income less 125.00 of cash expenses leaves 875.00.

Profit equals cash in this small example because both transactions are cash movements. A loan or unpaid sale would change that relationship. Check the company, dates and account classification before comparing reports; the reporting lesson works through the difference.

Which capabilities can you evaluate now?

Follow an invoice or bill through partial payment, credit and reconciliation, then enable optional stock workflows when needed.

  • Company setup and account management with audit history
  • Receipt, expense and general-journal drafts, posting, linked reversals
  • Account statements, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet
  • Cash scenario from your own assumptions

  • Ten supported functional currencies; one immutable functional currency per company/book
  • Shared customer/vendor parties, invoices, bills, partial payments, credits and historical ageing
  • Optional purchase orders, goods receipts, bill matching and received-but-unbilled reconciliation

Frequently asked questions

These answers describe the current release and the choices you need to make before evaluating it.

Is PHP Ledger really free?

The project-owned core is free under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Hosting, setup, optional support and a separate commercial licence have their own terms. The 0.1.0 through 0.1.5 previews retain their earlier MIT grants.

What do I need to run it?

Use PHP 8.2 or newer for both web and command-line requests, MySQL 8.4, HTTPS and terminal access. The package includes dependencies and an installation guide. PHP 8.3 is recommended.

Does it work on shared cPanel hosting?

A panel alone does not prove compatibility. Your plan must provide the required PHP extensions, MySQL 8.4, terminal access, private files and the correct public document root. Run package preflight on the actual account.

Does PHP Ledger send my data anywhere?

There are no licence-key or licensing-server calls. The static website has no analytics or form submission endpoint. The separate application has a documented optional country lookup; connections are explicit and scoped. Read the privacy page for the distinct website, demo and installed-application boundaries.

Can it invoice customers or settle bills yet?

Yes. The starter includes customer invoices, supplier bills, partial and final allocated payments, linked credits, ageing and control reconciliation. AR/AP belong to the core; owners can hide unused navigation. The separate cash POS remains a cash-sale showcase.

Is it ready for real books?

It remains a development preview. Technical tests and balanced reports are evidence for evaluation, but qualified accounting review, observed business use and supported statement packages remain open gates. Use synthetic records until you complete a reviewed acceptance process.

What if the project stops?

You retain the source and your self-hosted database under the applicable licence. Continued maintenance, recovery and data extraction still need an operator. Keep tested backups and portable report exports rather than relying on the existence of a ZIP alone.
