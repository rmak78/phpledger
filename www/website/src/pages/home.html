Double-entry accounting that runs on your own PHP and MySQL hosting

PHP Ledger connects invoices, bills, payments and optional stock workflows to balanced journals, then shows the profit and loss, balance sheet and cash position behind them. It is open source, with new project-owned code under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and version {{site.release.version}} is ready to download.

What happens when a sale is posted?

Choose from the catalog, take the cash and print the receipt. The balanced entry records: 5.75 to cash, 5.75 to sales. Stock movement, tax and card payments are not included.

Compare the basket total with cash received and change before posting. The cart shown totals 5.75. In the next review step, 10.00 received leaves 4.25 change. This cash counter is useful for evaluating the posting path; it does not provide a tax invoice or a customer credit account.

Why can profit differ from cash?

Unpaid invoices, supplier bills, loans and stock can affect profit and cash at different times. The illustrated report uses Willow Corner Shop’s synthetic 2025 history. Check the company, dates and account classification before comparing reports; the reporting lesson explains the distinction.

Which capabilities can you evaluate now?

Follow an invoice or bill through partial payment, credit and reconciliation, then enable optional stock workflows when needed.

  • Company setup and account management with audit history
  • Receipt, expense and general-journal drafts, posting, linked reversals
  • Account statements, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet
  • Cash scenario from your own assumptions
  • Cash point of sale with a printable receipt

Frequently asked questions

These answers describe the current release and the choices you need to make before evaluating it.

Is PHP Ledger really free?

The project-owned core is free under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Hosting, setup, optional support and a separate commercial licence have their own terms. The 0.1.0 through 0.1.5 previews retain their earlier MIT grants.

What do I need to run it?

Use PHP 8.2 or newer for both web and command-line requests, MySQL 8.4 and HTTPS. Installation is done in the browser at /install; no terminal access is required for setup. PHP 8.3 is recommended, and the PHP zip extension is needed for automatic updates.

Does it work on shared cPanel hosting?

A panel alone does not prove compatibility. Your plan must provide the required PHP extensions, MySQL 8.4, private files and the correct public document root. Run the browser installer's checks, or CLI preflight, on the actual account.

Does PHP Ledger send my data anywhere?

There are no licence-key or licensing-server calls. The static website has no analytics or form submission endpoint. The separate application has a documented optional country lookup; connections are explicit and scoped. Read the privacy page for the distinct website, demo and installed-application boundaries.

Can it invoice customers or settle bills yet?

Yes. The starter includes customer invoices, supplier bills, partial and final allocated payments, linked credits, ageing and control reconciliation. AR/AP belong to the core; owners can hide unused navigation. The separate cash POS remains a cash-sale showcase.

Is it ready for real books?

1.0.0 is the first stable release, backed by automated tests, fault-injection update/recovery tests and exact-artifact install/upgrade checks. Independent accounting review, independent security review and observed supervised-pilot use have not yet happened; the owner published with these limits disclosed as post-release commitments. Use synthetic records until you complete your own reviewed acceptance process.

What if the project stops?

You retain the source and your self-hosted database under the applicable licence. Continued maintenance, recovery and data extraction still need an operator. Keep tested backups and portable report exports rather than relying on the existence of a ZIP alone.
