Double-entry accounting that runs on your own PHP and MySQL hosting

PHP Ledger records receipts, expenses and cash sales as balanced journals, then shows the profit and loss, balance sheet and cash position behind them. It is open source, with new code under the MIT licence, and version {{site.release.version}} is ready to download.

A cash sale at the counter becomes a receipt and a balanced journal

Choose from the catalog, take the cash, print the receipt. The sale posts through the same service as every other entry: 5.75 to cash, 5.75 to sales. No stock, tax or card payments yet, on purpose.

See the point of sale {{icon:arrow-right}}

Each expense keeps its payee, date, category and journal together

Save a draft first. Nothing touches the books until you post it, and posting creates one balanced entry: 125.00 to general expenses, 125.00 from cash and bank. A correction is a linked reversal, so the history stays complete.

How the books work {{icon:arrow-right}}

At the end of the day the owner sees 875.00 in the bank and knows why

1,000.00 came in and 125.00 went out. The profit and loss, balance sheet and trial balance read the same posted entries, and every figure drills back to the journal and the record behind it.

What is in the preview, and what comes next

The current package covers connected accounts, general journals, daily transactions and reports. Further capabilities follow the roadmap and need accounting and usability review. Contributions, worked accounting examples and installation reports are welcome on GitHub.
