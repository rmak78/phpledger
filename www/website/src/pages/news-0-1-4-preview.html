Clearer financial reports in 0.1.4-preview

15 September 2026 · Layout correction

Income, Expenses, Assets, Liabilities and Equity tables now fit inside their report panels.

Reports that fit

An account-ledger table style was also affecting the smaller financial-report tables. This correction separates those styles, extends the ledger's complete-entry layout to tablets and keeps account filters inside their panel. The accounting calculations remain the same.

What was checked

Targeted browser checks covered seven routes at six widths from 320 to 1440 pixels, including account selection, running balances, date filters and horizontal scrolling. The package also passed a fresh installation and central posting checks.

This remains an evaluation preview with the 0.1.3 core and module capabilities. Richer synthetic multi-year company examples are planned. API/MCP reads remain next, followed by controlled commands and optional AR/AP. Technical checks do not replace qualified accounting review.
