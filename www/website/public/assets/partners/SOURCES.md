# Company logo sources

Verified 14 September 2026. These companies are presented as supporting PHP Ledger's open-source initiative at the project owner's request. The marks are used to identify and link to those companies; no customer carousel or portfolio entry was treated as evidence of affiliation. Logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. No open-source license for these marks is asserted.

The BixiTech, BixiSoft and Agency75 artwork sources are referenced by their current official homepages. BrownBag's company LinkedIn page at https://pk.linkedin.com/company/brownbagpk links to https://brownbag.pk/; that reachable official site uses the same brown/green .pk shopping-bag identity. Its current footer asset, https://brownbag.pk/assets/WhatsApp_Image_2025-11-13_at_8.45.42_PM-removebg-preview.png, was compared visually with the higher-resolution wide rendition hosted by BixiSoft. The wide rendition is used here. The owner explicitly requested BrownBag's inclusion as a supporter; the BixiSoft carousel alone was not used to establish that relationship.

The local WebPs are proportionally resized and compressed from the original artwork. No logo was generated, redrawn, recolored, stretched or combined into another logo. Original source files and their hashes are retained in the project's design evidence. The public `manifest.json` records derivative dimensions, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. All four logos load from this website; outbound company links load only when the visitor selects them.
