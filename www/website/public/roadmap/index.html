The path from a working preview to supported books

The published package has a bounded accounting and cash-sale journey. Each next step needs reconciled examples, access checks and review before it becomes a supported capability.

This roadmap describes development scope, not a delivery-date or regulatory-compliance promise.

Development sequence

  1. Available

    0.1.2-preview

    Company setup, audited account management, account statements, receipt and expense drafts, general journals, linked reversals, owner reports and a sample cash POS. One base currency per book, English interface.

    Download the evaluation package

    Published preview
  2. Next accounting work

    Opening balances and cutover

    Preview and validate opening positions at an explicit cutover date. Reconcile receivable and payable controls against unpaid documents before confirming. Keep source references and prevent duplicate opening entries.

    Development sequence 1
  3. Then

    Period administration

    Review fiscal periods, close and reopen with permission and a traceable reason. Keep period changes consistent with the central posting service and protect closed history.

    Development sequence 2
  4. Then

    Bank reconciliation

    Review statement rows, duplicates and ambiguous matches. Confirm reconciliation explicitly and retain a statement-to-journal audit trail. Regional bank import formats need actual sample exports and separate testing.

    Development sequence 3
  5. Accounting MVP

    Subledgers and reviewed reporting

    Customer and vendor balances, receivables, payables, historical imports and reviewed period-end statements. Recognition, measurement, classifications and disclosures need a qualified reviewer for the supported entity profile.

    Pending gates
  6. Regional work

    Pakistan, then the UK and UAE

    Versioned entity and accounting-framework profiles, documented policies and reviewed country adapters. A selected currency does not establish accounting or tax compliance.

    Research and review
  7. Later

    Inventory, retail and distribution

    Purchasing, stock valuation and cost of sales before a production retail expansion. Returns, settlement, hardware, distribution and specialist industries each need scoped pilots.

    Future milestones
  8. Later

    Languages, exchange rates and additional books

    Reviewed translations, flexible formats, traceable exchange-rate posting and clearly defined alternative accounting books. Offline operation and document scanning require separate design and acceptance work.

    Future milestones

Help test a real task

Useful contributions include synthetic worked accounting examples, installation results, reproducible bugs and feedback from owners and bookkeepers. Independent security, accounting and observed usability review remain open gates.

Frequently asked questions

When will a stable release be available?

There is no committed stable-release date. A supported release needs reconciled accounting examples, qualified accounting review, observed usability, installation and recovery checks, and clear support scope.

Does choosing a country or currency activate local accounting rules?

No. Each book uses one base currency. Country and currency choices do not activate tax calculations, statutory reporting, foreign-currency posting or additional accounting books. Regional accounting work follows the Pakistan, UK and UAE research and review sequence.

How can I help shape the next release?

Share a synthetic workflow example, installation report or reproducible issue through the project repository. Accountants, business owners and contributors can also use the Support page to prepare an email about review or pilot participation.
